Projects

Selected work

Problem

Manual releases required scheduled maintenance windows. Rollbacks were slow and often manual, and every deploy risked customer-facing downtime.

Solution

Built a Jenkins pipeline feeding GitOps-based progressive rollouts through ArgoCD onto Kubernetes, with automated canary analysis and instant, one-click rollback.

Outcome

Deploys now ship continuously during business hours with no visible impact to end users, across every production service.

92% reduction in deployment time — zero customer-facing downtime across 200+ releases

Problem

Infrastructure was provisioned ad hoc across AWS and Azure, leading to configuration drift, inconsistent environments, and slow onboarding for new services.

Solution

Designed a reusable Terraform module library covering networking, compute, and IAM, enforced through CI-driven plan validation and policy checks.

Outcome

New environments are now provisioned from a single, auditable source of truth instead of manual console work.

Provisioning time cut from ~2 weeks to 2 days — drift eliminated across 30+ environments

Problem

Incidents were most often first detected by customer reports rather than internal alerting, with logs scattered across services and no shared source of truth.

Solution

Deployed a unified stack — Prometheus for metrics, Loki for logs, Grafana for dashboards — with SLO-based alert thresholds tuned to cut noise.

Outcome

On-call now sees actionable alerts tied to real user impact, with correlated logs and metrics in one place.

Mean-time-to-detect cut by 74% — from ~32 minutes to under 8 minutes

Problem

Node and pod failures required manual on-call intervention, generating repeated after-hours pages for well-understood, recoverable failure modes.

Solution

Implemented layered health checks, pod disruption budgets, and ArgoCD-managed recovery manifests, templated through Helm for consistency across clusters.

Outcome

Common failure classes now resolve themselves within minutes, with no page sent unless recovery genuinely requires a human.

Automated recovery from 95% of common failures — after-hours pages down 60%
